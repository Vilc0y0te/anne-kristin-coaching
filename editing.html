How to edit this website

You have an assistant who knows every corner of this site. You describe what you want in normal words, in English or German, and it makes the change and publishes it. No code, ever.

The whole process, three steps

01

Open a session

Go to claude.ai/code (bookmark it), choose the website project anne-kristin-coaching, and start a new session.

02

Say what you want

Type it like a message to a colleague. Paste a screenshot of the part you mean, that always helps. One change at a time works best.

03

Check the live site

The assistant publishes automatically. Wait one minute, then refresh innernarrativecoach.org. On the phone, use an incognito tab to be sure you see the newest version.

What to say, examples that work

Changing text

  • "On the homepage, change the headline to: Meet the story beneath your story."
  • "Add this new testimonial from Sarah, a designer from Berlin: [paste the text]. Put it next to Kelly's."
  • "The price of the Reading changes to S$520 everywhere on the site."
  • "Rewrite the About page introduction, warmer, and mention my new certification: [details]."

Layout and design

  • "The swan section feels cramped on my phone, give it more breathing room." (attach a screenshot)
  • "Make the testimonials appear before the packages on the homepage."
  • "The buttons on the quiz page are too small on mobile."

Navigation

  • "Rename 'Work With Me' to 'Offers' in the menu, on every page."
  • "Add the Archetype Library to the top menu."
  • "Remove the Podcast link from the menu but keep the page reachable from the footer."

Features (calendar, quiz, forms)

  • "My Calendly link changed, the new one is: [link]. Update the booking page."
  • "Change question 7 of the quiz to: [new question and answers]."
  • "Add a field 'How did you hear about me?' to the interest form."
  • "The discovery call is 30 minutes now, update the texts."

Adding pictures

  • Paste or attach the image directly in the chat, then: "Add this photo to the About page, next to the section about the podcast."
  • "Replace the swan image with this one." (attach it)
  • "This portrait goes on the Fantasy Portraits section as a new example."

Publishing content

  • "Add this LinkedIn post to the Journal: [paste text and the LinkedIn link]."
  • "Write a Journal article about the Explorer archetype based on my library page, around 600 words, my voice."

Five rules of thumb

  • Screenshots beat descriptions. If you can see the problem, photograph it and paste it.
  • One change per request. "Fix the logo" then "now the testimonials" works better than five things at once.
  • Say where. "On the homepage", "on the booking page", "in the menu". The site has 25 pages.
  • You cannot break anything permanently. Every change is saved with history. If something looks wrong, just say: "Undo the last change."
  • Give it a minute. Publishing takes about 60 seconds. If you still see the old version, refresh hard or use an incognito tab.
