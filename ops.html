The operating rhythm

  • Daily (10 min): open Today, clear "Needs reply". Every lead gets a personal answer within 24 hours; speed is the cheapest trust you will ever buy.
  • Monday (15 min): This week view: send due follow-ups, welcome the week's new Letter subscribers in one batch.
  • First Tuesday (45 min): the Inner Letter: personalise the draft, one true client story, send.
  • Follow-up doctrine: exactly one follow-up per interest registration, 7 days later, leading with a gift (a relevant Journal piece), never with a push. One for teams after 3 days. After that, they live on the Letter, which follows up for you every month without pressure.
  • Never: more than one ask per email; a follow-up without something of value in it; a reply that could have been written by anyone.
